Mimi

A private assistant that runs on your own computer.

Chat with it, and let it help with your calendar, your email and your reminders. It remembers what you tell it, and keeps all of it on your computer.

What it does

One assistant for the everyday things.

Connect only what you want. Everything else keeps working without it.

Chat that remembers

Talk to it the way you'd talk to a person. It learns from what you tell it, like names, habits and preferences. You can see, change or delete anything it remembers.

Your calendar

Google Calendar, iCloud, Fastmail or Nextcloud. See your week, ask what's coming up, and have it add or move events for you.

Your email

It sorts new mail into what needs a reply, what's important and the rest, sums up each conversation in a line, and drafts replies you can edit. Works with Gmail, iCloud, Fastmail, Proton Mail and more.

Reminders and routines

"Remind me to call the dentist on Monday." Reminders can repeat or come before an event. Routines run on a schedule, like a rundown of your day every morning.

On your phone, through Telegram

Connect your own Telegram bot and talk to Mimi when you're out. Reminders reach you there, and you can approve what it wants to do with a tap.

The people in your life

Your contacts and the people you email, in one list. Type @ and a name to ask about someone, and it knows who you mean.

Private by design

Your data stays with you.

Mimi is an app on your computer, not a website. The people who make it never see your conversations, because there's nowhere for them to go.

Runs on your computer

Conversations, memories, email and calendars are kept on your own machine.

Local AI models by default

Mimi looks at your computer, suggests a model that fits, downloads it and runs it itself. No extra software to install.

No Mimi server, no account, no tracking

The project runs no servers. There's nothing to sign up for, no analytics and no telemetry. Mimi talks to a service like Google or Telegram only when you use it, and straight from your computer.

Cloud models only if you choose

On an older computer, a cloud model may work better. You can pick one, and Mimi shows it everywhere it's in use, so you always know.

Encrypted on disk

Your conversations, memories, mail and passwords are kept in an encrypted database on your computer.

It asks before it acts

Before it sends an email or changes your calendar, it shows you exactly what it's about to do and waits for your OK. You decide what, if anything, it may do without asking.

Get Mimi

Install it with one line.

For macOS and Linux. Mimi brings everything it needs, so there's nothing else to set up.

The quickest way

Open Terminal, paste this line and press Return:

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TisaneFruitRouge/mimi/main/scripts/install.sh | sh

It picks the right installer for your computer from GitHub, checks it and installs it. Run it again later to update. Mimi doesn't look for updates on its own unless you turn that on.

Or download an installer

Pick the one for your computer. It's always the latest version.

All versions
  • Mac with Apple siliconM1 and later · .dmg
  • Mac with Intel.dmg
  • Debian, Ubuntu.deb
  • Fedora, openSUSE.rpm
  • Any LinuxAppImage

Linux builds are for 64-bit Intel and AMD processors. The Mac app isn't notarized by Apple yet, so the first time you open it, right-click Mimi and choose Open.

Not sure which Mac you have? Apple menu › About This Mac: "Chip" means Apple silicon, "Processor" means Intel.

Free and open source
